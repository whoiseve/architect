Adaptive Acoustic Environment System

The room becomes
a controllable
parameter.

AAES uses real-time acoustic telemetry to dynamically adjust the physical behavior of the live room. Engineers no longer guess the space — they work with measurable, repeatable acoustic data.

Motorized ceiling clouds, deployable GOBO wall systems, variable bass traps, and a continuous measurement network combine into a system as recallable as a plugin in a DAW.
